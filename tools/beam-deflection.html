Beam Deflection Calculator

Midspan deflection of a simply supported beam under a uniform or point load.

About this calculator

Simply supported, linear elastic beam. Uniform load: δ = 5wL⁴ ÷ 384EI. Midspan point load: δ = PL³ ÷ 48EI. The result is compared with span ÷ 360, a common serviceability reference.
