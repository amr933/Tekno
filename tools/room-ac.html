Room AC Sizing Calculator

Cooling capacity (BTU/h and HP) for a room from area, sun, insulation, occupancy and floor.

About this calculator

Enter the room area and conditions. The tool applies a 550 BTU/h per m² base with factors for sun, insulation, occupancy and a top-floor roof, then recommends the next standard unit size.
