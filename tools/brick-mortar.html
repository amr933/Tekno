Brick & Mortar Calculator

Brick count plus laying-mortar and plaster quantities for a wall area.

About this calculator

Defaults are the Egyptian red brick 20×10×6 cm with a 10 mm joint. Mortar is wall volume minus brick volume; plaster is area × sides × thickness.
