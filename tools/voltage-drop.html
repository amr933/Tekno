Cable Voltage Drop Calculator

Check voltage drop along a copper or aluminium cable for single-phase and three-phase systems.

About this calculator

Enter the nominal voltage, current, one-way cable length, conductor size and material. The tool uses resistance and reactance per metre to estimate the drop and the voltage at the load end, then compares the percentage with a common 3 percent guideline.
